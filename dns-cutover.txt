NORYX.IO — DNS CUTOVER SHEET
Prepared 14 Sep 2026. Target: the new site on Vercel (project "noryx").

WHY THIS SHEET EXISTS
The DNS zone for noryx.io currently lives in a Netlify account you cannot
reach. Moving the domain to Namecheap's own DNS is what frees it. But that
move wipes every record the old zone held, and two of those records are your
email. Recreate all of them or info@noryx.io stops receiving mail.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1  Namecheap -> Domain List -> noryx.io -> MANAGE -> NAMESERVERS
        Change "Custom DNS" to "Namecheap BasicDNS" and save.
        The old site goes down at this moment. That is expected.

STEP 2  Go to the "Advanced DNS" tab.
        DELETE any record Namecheap adds by itself, usually:
          - CNAME  www  -> parkingpage.namecheap.com
          - URL Redirect Record on @
        Then add everything below, exactly.

WORTH ADDING WHILE YOU ARE IN THERE
Your domain has no SPF and no DMARC record today. That is a pre-existing gap,
not something the move caused. Without SPF, anyone can send mail that claims to
come from noryx.io, and your own mail is more likely to land in spam. These two
records fix it and are the standard Google Workspace values:

  Type            Host    Value
  TXT Record      @       v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
  TXT Record      _dmarc  v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:info@noryx.io

Add them only if you want to. Nothing else on this sheet depends on them.
