Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains what Noryx collects when you use noryx.io, who processes it on our behalf, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who this policy covers

Noryx Technologies ("Noryx", "we") operates the website noryx.io. This policy covers the website only. Data you give us inside a signed engineering project is governed by that project's contract, not by this page.

2. What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive exactly the fields you filled in:

  • Name
  • Company (optional)
  • Email address
  • Site location (optional)
  • Your message

We do not ask for a phone number on the form. If you contact us by WhatsApp or by phone instead, we see the number you are calling from.

If — and only if — you accept measurement cookies, Google Analytics and an OpenAI measurement pixel record standard technical data: approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, the pages you viewed and how you arrived. Choose "Essential only" and neither script is downloaded at all.

3. What stays on your own device

Two items are written to your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to us on their own:

  • noryx_cookie_consent — the cookie choice you made, so you are not asked again on every visit.
  • noryx_pending_lead — if a form submission fails (no signal, for example), what you typed is kept on your device so it is not lost, and is cleared the moment the message goes through.

You can delete both at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

4. Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, and keep a record of what was discussed if the enquiry becomes a project. We do not use contact form details for marketing lists, and we do not send newsletters.

5. Who else processes it

We do not sell personal data and we do not trade it. These are the service providers that necessarily handle it, and what each one does:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) — receives the contact form and forwards it to our inbox.
  • Vercel Inc. (United States) — hosts this website and serves it over a global content delivery network.
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Google Analytics, and Google Workspace which carries our email.
  • OpenAI — a measurement pixel that records whether a visit resulted in an enquiry.
  • WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. (Meta) — only if you choose to open a WhatsApp conversation with us.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Israel, including in the United States and the European Union. By sending us a message you accept that the message is handled on those servers.

6. Cookies and your choice

On your first visit a banner asks how you want to be measured. Nothing beyond what the page needs to function loads before you answer. "Essential only" means no analytics or measurement script is fetched — not that it is fetched and ignored. To change your mind later, clear site data for noryx.io in your browser and the banner will appear again.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Enquiries that become projects are kept for as long as the engagement and the record-keeping periods that follow it require. Analytics data is retained according to the provider's own default period.

8. Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is wrong or incomplete, and ask us to delete it. Write to office@noryx.io and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Form submissions travel encrypted. Access to the enquiry inbox is limited to Noryx staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

10. Children

This is a business-to-business site. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Continued use of the site after that date means the updated policy applies.
